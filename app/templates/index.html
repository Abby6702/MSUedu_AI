How it works

  1. Find a tutor. Search by subject, topic or day, or use guided matching. You only see tutors an administrator has approved.
  2. Request a session. Review your request, then confirm. The tutor accepts, declines or suggests another time. Nothing is booked twice.
  3. Learn and reflect. Practise with AI-written quizzes, ask the AI assistant, and read your tutor's session notes.

Who is it for?

Students

Find tutors, request sessions, practise with quizzes, and use an AI coordinator that prepares bookings for your confirmation.

Peer tutors

Share your subjects and weekly availability, manage requests, and record what each session covered.

Administrators

Review tutors, manage accounts and subjects, monitor requests and moderate feedback.
