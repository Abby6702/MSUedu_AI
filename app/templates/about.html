About MSUEdu AI

MSUEdu AI is a student project that demonstrates an AI-assisted peer tutoring platform. It is not an official website of Mindanao State University and is not endorsed by it.

How the AI is used

  • Tutors may use an AI helper during sessions to draft exercises, explanations and summaries. They type only the topic and their own notes, never your name. You only see a session summary after your tutor reviews it and chooses to share it. It does not feed the recommendation rules, which use only your quiz results and completed lessons.
    • By default the AI runs on the server's own computer (through Ollama), so your questions stay on that computer. If the administrator switches to an online AI provider, your questions and lesson text are sent to that provider.
    • The AI tutor, quizzes and session recaps are generated text and can contain mistakes. Check important information with your textbook, instructor or tutor.
    • The AI tutor is designed to help you understand, with explanations, examples and hints, not to hand you answers to submit.
    • The tutoring coordinator only prepares requests and cancellations. Nothing happens until you press Confirm, and it can never accept a request on a tutor's behalf.
    • The learning recommendations come from fixed rules over your quiz results and completed lessons. They do not read your AI conversations.
    • Quiz scores and AI activity counts are not proof that you have mastered a topic.

    What is stored

  • Live session chat messages are saved with the session and can be read by the student and the tutor of that session. Video and audio go directly between your browsers and are not recorded or stored. A connection helper (a STUN server, and a TURN relay if one is configured) can see technical details such as network addresses.
  • Whiteboard drawings are kept only while the live room is open and are not saved afterwards. Shared files are stored on the server and can be downloaded only by the student and the tutor of that session. People who manage the server could technically access them.
  • By default tutors cannot see your quiz results. If you switch on Sharing with tutors, tutors you have a confirmed or completed session with can see your topic averages, lesson progress and learning goals. They never see your AI conversations or individual answers.
    • Your account (name, email, a scrambled password hash), your profile, requests, sessions, quiz attempts, lesson progress, feedback and notifications.
    • Your AI tutor conversations are saved so you can continue them. In the app only you can see them and you can delete them at any time. People who manage the database could technically read it.
    • Coordinator messages and notes you paste for summaries are not saved. Only counts (which feature, success or failure) are kept.
    • Tutor student numbers are visible only to administrators. Feedback is shown without the student's name.
    • Important administrator actions are recorded in an audit log, without passwords.

    Please note

    • Do not type passwords or private information into forms or AI tools.
    • Content marked "Sample" is fictional demonstration data.
    • This page is a plain-language summary, not a legal privacy policy. Before using real student data, the project needs permission, a proper privacy review and appropriate safeguards.
